NETWORK-SIZE THREAT INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------
Absolute spread under no defense:
  4 agents: 1.90 reached agents/run
  20 agents: 1.83 reached agents/run
  Descriptively, larger networks expose FEWER agents in absolute terms under no defense.

Relative spread under no defense:
  4 agents: 63.39% of non-seed agents reached
  20 agents: 9.62% of non-seed agents reached
  Descriptively, the worm occupies a SMALLER fraction of the network as network size increases from the smallest to the largest evaluated setting, even if absolute reach may increase.

Highest mean no-defense reach fraction: 4 agents (63.39%).
Deepest mean no-defense propagation: 20 agents (depth 1.62).

WormShield at the largest evaluated network:
  Residual attack reach: 0.08%
  Mean reach reduction versus no defense: 9.54 percentage points
  Fully contained by WormShield: 98.46% of malicious runs

TOPOLOGY THREAT INTERPRETATION
------------------------------
Largest no-defense reach fraction: Sparse mesh (41.86%).
Smallest no-defense reach fraction: Star (16.48%).
Deepest propagation: Sparse mesh (mean max depth 2.07).
Most successful malicious relays: Sparse mesh (3.89 relays/run).

WormShield highest containment by topology: Random min-degree (98.46%).
WormShield lowest containment by topology: Ring (89.67%).

CAUTION
-------
These are descriptive held-out trace results, not a causal network-size scaling law. Network size and topology co-vary with realized prompts, roles, and propagation opportunities in AgentArena.
Do not claim that size alone causes higher or lower threat unless a future controlled experiment holds all other factors fixed.
